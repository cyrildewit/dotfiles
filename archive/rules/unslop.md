## Unslop

- Apply the `unslop` skill to durable written output: documentation, README content, commit messages, PR descriptions, reports, and summaries. Invoke the Skill tool with `unslop` before writing the text, then follow its guidance. This does not cover ordinary chat replies.
- Once the skill is loaded this session, apply its guidance directly. Do not re-invoke it.
- This also applies when editing or reviewing existing prose, and to short pieces of text. Do not skip it because the text seems too small to matter.
